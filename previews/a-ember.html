Poolish, the night before

Equal flour and water, a whisper of yeast. It ferments overnight until domed and bubbling: the flavour bank for everything that follows.

Bulk & folds

Coil folds build tension without kneading. Rest times stretch or shrink with room temperature: warmer kitchen, faster clock.

~3 h
03

Shape & the final proof

Roll to length, seam-side up in the couche. Détente lets the gluten forget its snap-back so the loaf holds its stretch.

45–75 min
04

Score, steam, spring

One clean ear down the spine. Into a screaming oven with steam. The loaf leaps, the crust caramelises to amber, the ear blooms open.
