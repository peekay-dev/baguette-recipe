Watch dough become Baguette

Straight off the rack: poolish sourdough baguettes, scored by hand and sprung in a home oven. This is the loaf the method below is built to produce.

Six stages, one living dough.

01

Proof

A slack ball of poolish dough, breathing as wild-slow yeast gasses it up.

12–16 h
02

Shape

Pre-shaped, rested, then rolled out long under flat palms into a knobbly, floured torpedo.

~20 min
03

Score

One long, confident slash down the spine with a wet lame, feathering as the skin tears.

seconds
04

Oven spring

Trapped gas expands violently in the heat. The score bursts open into a raised ear.

first 8 min
05

Bake

Sugars caramelise, the crust deepens amber, the tips darken, steam sets the shine.

22 min total
06

Baguette

Crackling crust, open crumb. It sings as it cools on the rack.

cool 30 min
