## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment (A)**: Low-to-moderate slop risk; personality is present but unevenly distributed. Genuine craft signals — physics-derived times in plain language, the flour source-pill + "↺ reset" mechanic, a procedurally-staggered baguette SVG, Hedvig Letters Serif vs Figtree pairing. Composition is not template-grade: terracotta header, staged-pill nav, and content panels each hold a distinct register. Where slop shows: the `tip()` closed state reads as a caption not a control; the "You made baguettes." beat is inert; the flour A/B split (select + % input ×2) is compositionally dense and ungrouped.

**Deterministic scan (B)**: `detect.mjs` exited 0 with an empty findings array — **but the CSS-cascade engine never ran**. `htmlparser2` / `css-select` / `css-tree` / `domutils` are not installed, so the detector silently fell back to regex-only matching. Zero findings here is closer to "unanalyzed by the structural engine" than "certified clean." Assessment B compensated with a manual rule-by-rule pass over all 1,300 lines: no `background-clip:text` gradient text (the `#crust`/`#minicrust` gradients are SVG loaf fills — a future SVG-aware detector could false-positive these), no overused/single font, healthy 4.1:1 type-size range, no bounce easing, no dark glow, no hero-eyebrow chip, no repeated section kickers, no side-stripe borders, no justified text, no layout-property transitions (grid-template-rows collapse is a valid pattern). Manual verdict: no anti-patterns present.

## Overall Impression

A capable, trustworthy, domain-specific tool with a genuinely original core idea (physics-derived times surfaced as provenance pills). Since the last pass the blend slider got its label and a celebration line appeared, but the two structural drags are unchanged: the help system still fragments across up to four disclosure affordances, and the accessibility cluster (roles, aria-labels, AT-invisible toggle state) is entirely unaddressed. Fix disclosure consistency and the a11y roles and this clears 32.

## What's Working

1. **Source pill + "↺ reset" mechanic** — renders flour-derived provenance inline in the field label and swaps to a reset control when overridden. Surfaces derivation at the point of decision without a modal. Nothing comparable in the category. The app's single most distinctive concept.
2. **Sticky stage strip + deterministic scroll-spy + big-to-mini baguette handoff** — the full animated loaf in the header hands off to a mini steaming loaf in the stage bar as the header scrolls away; the `bar-loaf-enter` spring (scale 0→1.12→1) is polished and the scroll-spy explicitly solves the IntersectionObserver gap.
3. **Physics surfaced as teaching** — `solveStageDuration()` fixed-point iteration, Newton cooling, Q10 yeast activity all appear in tip copy with concrete numbers. The DDT worked example ("96 − 54 − 2°C friction = 40°C") is the best inline teaching moment in the app.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Up to four competing disclosure patterns (still open, count went up)**
- **Why it matters**: Each disclosure affordance is a separate learned behavior. Tips use `+/−` as a `::before` prefix; the Schedule header uses `+/−` as a `::after` suffix — the same symbol on opposite sides of the same interaction — while Method uses ⓘ modals and Advanced uses a `▾` chevron. On a phone with floury hands the inconsistency doubles cognitive overhead.
- **Fix**: Consolidate to two patterns: a `▾` chevron for every expanding content section (tips, Schedule, Advanced — move `.tip-toggle` from `::before +` to a right-aligned `::after` chevron matching `.adv-toggle`), and ⓘ modals reserved only for "why this number" explanations that warrant isolation.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Tip toggle has no full-weight label in its closed state (new finding)**
- **Why it matters**: `.tip-toggle` renders only the `+` and a `.tip-hint` span at `0.75em / opacity 0.7`. The hint string is doing primary-label work at subhead weight (~11px light gray on mobile), so users may not register tips as interactive at all — the help content is one washed-out tap target away from invisible.
- **Fix**: Either bump `.tip-hint` to `font-weight:600 / opacity:1`, or add a real label parameter to `tip()` rendered at full weight with the hint as a smaller subline.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] End-of-recipe celebration is present but inert (partially fixed)**
- **Why it matters**: "You made baguettes." was added, but at `1.3rem / var(--accent) / 400` with a thin top border it is styled identically to every h2 — it reads as a section divider, not a peak-end beat. After a two-day bake the last emotional memory is a baking instruction followed by a faint heading.
- **Fix**: Drop the existing `.bar-loaf` SVG (steam, gradient crust, score marks) into this panel, scaled up, with an entrance animation on scroll-into-view and celebration text at 1.6–1.8rem. The animation infrastructure already exists — this is composition, not new code.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable delight`

**[P2] Stage-bar and header text contrast is borderline at small sizes**
- **Why it matters**: Inactive/done stage pills use `color: var(--accent-ink); opacity: 0.45/0.65` composited over the terracotta `--accent`. The inactive blend lands ≈4.6:1 on 0.68rem (~10.9px) bold text — right at the edge, and "done" pills drop lower. Compositing reduced-opacity light text over a saturated background is the fragile part.
- **Fix**: Replace opacity-composited foreground with concrete hex colors verified ≥4.5:1 (raise inactive toward `rgba(247,236,228,0.7)`+), and dim "done" pills via element opacity including border rather than text alone.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit`

**[P3] Flour dropdown jargon still blocks first-timers (still open)**
- **Why it matters**: "Caputo Nuvola (13%)" assumes the reader knows 13% is protein and Nuvola is a brand with specific extensibility. The flour choice drives hydration and development, so a blind pick cascades into a confusing recipe.
- **Fix**: Add plain descriptors ("Caputo Pizzeria Blue · 12.5% protein · extensible, chewy") or `<optgroup>` by "Beginner-friendly" / "Italian / Specialty", and label the percentage as protein.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable onboard`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile)**: (1) `.tip-hint` label ~11px light gray is near-illegible at arm's length despite a 44px target. (2) Flour-blend row packs two selects + two `%` inputs + slider + readout into a 375px width; `%` inputs shrink to ~72px. (3) Modal close `×` is ~22px — below the 44px tap minimum, frequent mis-taps.

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: (1) Hero `72% hydration` / `4×250g · cold retard · 18°C` reads as status, not editable settings. (2) "Fermentolyse" step name has no plain synonym at the title. (3) Source pill "Yitpi" (wheat variety, not brand) appears next to Hydration with zero explanation — reads as "a setting I shouldn't touch." (4) The `τ` INFO modal uses `T_amb / T_fridge / T_target` engineering notation.

**Sam (Accessibility)**: (1) `.tip` elements are `tabindex=0` with no `role="button"` — announced as generic containers. (2) Method compare button has `title="Compare methods"` but no `aria-label` (title is not reliably announced). (3) Stage pills are `onclick` spans with no `role`/`aria-current`/`aria-pressed` — no way to tell "now" from "done". (4) Fold `−/+` steppers lack the aria-labels the weight/count steppers have. (5) Schedule `::after` `+/−` and `modal-close ×` are AT-invisible / announced as "multiplication sign".

## Minor Observations

- `src-folds` pill always shows the flour name even with no override, while the hydration pill hides on default — inconsistent pill behavior on the same form.
- `hero-flour` "blend" label is identical for 95/5 and 50/50 splits — the word carries no ratio information.
- Step 6 (Final Shape) renders with an empty time slot — no timing anchor on a step that sits between timed stages.
- No `<meta name="theme-color">` — Android Chrome keeps white browser chrome above the terracotta header, breaking the header-as-brand effect.
- `localStorage` key `baguette_prefs` is unversioned; a schema change silently mixes stale prefs with new defaults.
- Modal overlay toggles `display:none`→`flex` with no transition, slightly jarring against the rest of the motion system.

## Questions to Consider

- The source-pill mechanic is the app's best idea but is never explained in-page. Should a first-time pill display trigger a one-time "suggested by your flour profile — tap to learn why" annotation?
- Baguette-making isn't daily; on return after a month do stale localStorage prefs help or confuse? Is a visible top-of-Controls "reset to defaults" warranted alongside the per-pill resets?
- Schedule — the one output that answers "when do I start?" — is collapsed by default. Should it be the default-open panel with Formula collapsed?
- Step names mix plain ("Final Shape") and expert ("Fermentolyse", "Cold Retard"). Does title-level expertise signaling build trust or alienate the first-timer the controls form seems to also court?
